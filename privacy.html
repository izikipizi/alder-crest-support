Privacy Policy

Effective September 28, 2026

Alder Crest stores user-created simulated accounts and transactions locally. Vault files are encrypted locally before persistence. Album and item metadata are kept in app-private storage and are not sent to the developer.

On Apple devices, if encrypted iCloud backup is enabled, ciphertext, encrypted metadata, deletion state, and wrapped recovery-key material are stored in the user's private CloudKit database. Android vault content is stored locally and Android system cloud backup is disabled. The raw vault key, vault PIN or password, biometric data, simulated finance content, and plaintext vault content are not sent to the developer.

Photo, file, camera, and microphone access are used only for actions the user starts. Face ID, Touch ID, or Android biometric authentication is evaluated by the operating system; Alder Crest receives only the authentication result.

Free users may see Google AdMob banners on finance screens. Google User Messaging Platform gathers applicable advertising privacy choices before ad requests. Alder Crest does not intentionally send vault content, simulated finance content, credentials, or encryption material to Google. Google may process device, advertising, diagnostic, and consent information under its own policies.

Apple or Google processes purchase and entitlement information for one-time in-app purchases. Alder Crest reads verified store transactions to unlock purchased features.

For privacy requests or questions, email kip@cloud84.com.
