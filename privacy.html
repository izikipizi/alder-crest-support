Privacy Policy

Effective September 28, 2026

Alder Crest stores user-created simulated accounts and transactions locally. Vault files, authoritative filenames, album names, and album memberships are encrypted locally before persistence. A protected on-device search index stores normalized filename sort keys and keyed search tokens; it is not uploaded.

If encrypted iCloud backup is enabled, ciphertext, encrypted metadata, deletion state, and wrapped recovery-key material are stored in the user's private CloudKit database. The raw vault key, vault PIN or password, Face ID data, simulated finance content, and plaintext vault content are not sent to the developer.

Photos, Files, camera, and microphone access are used only for user-initiated imports or capture. Face ID is evaluated by iOS and Alder Crest receives only the authentication result.

Free users may see Google AdMob banners on finance screens. Google User Messaging Platform gathers applicable advertising privacy choices before ad requests. Alder Crest does not intentionally send vault content, simulated finance content, credentials, or encryption material to Google. Google may process device, advertising, diagnostic, and consent information under its own policies.

Apple processes purchase and entitlement information for one-time in-app purchases. Alder Crest reads verified StoreKit transactions to unlock purchased features.

For privacy requests or questions, email kip@cloud84.com.
