Support

Alder Crest keeps private photos, videos, and files in an encrypted vault behind a customizable simulated finance interface.

For help, email kip@cloud84.com. Include your iOS version and a description of the issue. Never email your vault PIN, password, or private files.

Common questions

  • Forgotten vault credential: Alder Crest cannot recover or view it. Use your verified iCloud recovery flow if previously configured.
  • Face ID: Enable Face ID in Alder Crest settings after creating your vault credential.
  • Purchases: Use Restore Purchases in Settings with the Apple Account that made the purchase.
  • Imported media: Originals stay in Photos or Files unless you delete them yourself.
Simulated finance interface. Alder Crest is not a bank, does not connect to financial institutions, does not hold money, and does not provide financial services.
